# Code of Conduct

RPG Narrative Engine should be a place where people can build, write, learn, disagree, and collaborate without harassment or contempt.

Be respectful, assume good faith until evidence says otherwise, critique work rather than people, respect boundaries, and make room for contributors with different experience levels and accessibility needs. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

For ordinary project disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request. For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the contact method on their GitHub profile and include links or screenshots where useful. Do not expose another person's private information in a public issue.
